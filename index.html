Sensitivity analysis & attribution

Cutting Through the Noise

A late delivery or a high electricity bill can have several plausible explanations. Learn to identify which inputs drive the outcome, question an uncertain ranking and decide what’s worth testing. Start with a data pipeline, then try a household budget.

Start lesson 1 Four lessons and independent practice · allow about 60–90 minutes

Before you start

Bring basic comfort reading a table and comparing numbers. The course teaches rank correlation, variance and prediction error as you need them. Everything runs in your browser.

Quick refresher on measurements
Latency
The time a job takes to finish. A second is 1,000 milliseconds (ms).
Median and p95
Half of outcomes are at or below the median. About 95 in 100 are at or below the 95th percentile (p95).
Cache hit rate
The share of requests served from the cache. A rise from 70% to 80% is 10 percentage points.
