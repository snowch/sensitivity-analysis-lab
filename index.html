Understand the queue rule and Sobol estimates

The queue adds 155 ms when arrival rate reaches 750 jobs/s and worker capacity is at most 800 jobs/s. Each condition covers one quarter of its input range. With independent inputs, the two hold together in about one run in sixteen. The abrupt penalty is an illustrative stand-in for a saturation regime.

First-order sensitivity estimates the variance explained by an input on its own. Total-order sensitivity also includes every interaction involving that input. Two inputs can share an interaction, so total-order estimates can sum to more than one. The estimates use independent uniform inputs and two sample matrices that exchange one column at a time. Small negative first-order values can occur from sampling noise.

Check what changed

Widening network RTT increases its contribution while its coefficient in the latency formula stays at 1. Measure the actual RTT range before investing in network work.

Under saturation, arrival rate and capacity dominate the Sobol total-order chart, even with modest rank correlations. Their first-order estimates are much smaller because the large delay depends on both. Cache hit rate still matters for typical runs. Investigate the queue when the latency tail is the problem.

What does the predictor rely on?

Measure how much held-out mean absolute error (MAE) rises after shuffling each feature or group.

Fit the model and shuffle features to see the change in error.

Inspect the dataset and evaluation

These synthetic logs include measurement noise, blanks in two columns and an irrelevant deploy-age column. Edge miss rate and origin fetch rate are noisy proxies for the same unlogged cache behaviour. Twelve other columns describe pipeline settings or operating conditions.

The browser shuffles rows once for a fixed 8,000/2,000 train/test split. It imputes missing entries with training medians, fits 24 bootstrap regression trees on the training rows, then measures error on the same held-out rows before and after each shuffle. The shuffle standard deviation quantifies variation across shuffle draws on this split. See the data generator, forest and permutation code.

Check the inference you can make

Compare both shuffle modes. When you shuffle just one cache counter, its partner can still carry similar information. Shuffling them together preserves their relationship with each other and disrupts their joint relationship with latency.

Importance describes the model’s reliance on its inputs. To test an intervention, replay the same burst workload and compare current worker capacity with increased capacity. Randomise the order across repeated runs, hold payload and cache policy steady, and compare p95 latency alongside resource cost.
