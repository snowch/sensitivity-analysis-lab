When the formula is unavailable, what can a predictor tell you about your logs?

Break the link between a signal and its row

First fit a model on 8,000 runs, then measure how far its predictions miss on 2,000 unseen runs. Mean absolute error (MAE) is the average size of those misses, in milliseconds.

Permutation importance shuffles the values in one test column, leaving the measured latencies and other columns in place. The model stays fixed. If its error rises, that column carried information the model used. A second column carrying similar information can cover for it, so try shuffling the two together.

Inspect the dataset and evaluation

These synthetic logs include measurement noise, blanks in two columns and an irrelevant deploy-age column. Edge miss rate and origin fetch rate are noisy proxies for the same unlogged cache behaviour. Twelve other columns describe pipeline settings or operating conditions.

The browser shuffles rows once for a fixed 8,000/2,000 train/test split. It imputes missing entries with training medians, fits 24 bootstrap regression trees on the training rows, then measures error on the same held-out rows before and after each shuffle. The shuffle standard deviation quantifies variation across shuffle draws on this split. See the data generator, forest and permutation code.

Use the method on observational data

Decide what new data your predictor will face. Split repeated observations by household, machine or customer when those groups might appear in both sets. For forecasting, reserve later periods for testing. Fit imputation and the model on training data, then compare the untouched test score with a simple baseline before attributing anything.

Choose an error metric for your decision. MAE describes a typical absolute miss averaged across all test rows. If extreme outcomes matter most, evaluate predictive quality in that regime as well. Repeat each shuffle and check stability across reasonable splits and model choices.

Correlated columns can substitute for each other. A row-wise shuffle can also create implausible combinations. Group features that represent the same measurement when the joint signal is the question. The resulting importance belongs to this model, dataset and score. A controlled change is a separate test.

Read the attribution result

Compare both shuffle modes. When you shuffle just one cache counter, its partner can still carry similar information. Shuffling them together preserves their relationship with each other and disrupts their joint relationship with latency.

Importance describes the model’s reliance on its inputs. To test an intervention, replay the same burst workload and compare current worker capacity with increased capacity. Randomise the order across repeated runs, hold payload and cache policy steady, and compare p95 latency alongside resource cost.
