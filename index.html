Before you start

You’ll read tables and compare numbers. The lessons introduce rank correlation, variance and prediction error as you need them. The experiments run in your browser.

Quick refresher on measurements
Latency
The time a job takes to finish. A second is 1,000 milliseconds (ms).
Median and p95
Half of outcomes are at or below the median. About 95 in 100 are at or below the 95th percentile (p95).
Cache hit rate
The share of requests served from the cache. A rise from 70% to 80% is 10 percentage points.
