Before you start

Bring basic arithmetic and comfort using a browser. Start by choosing an output, then calculate a change by hand. The lessons introduce sampling and statistics when you need them. All essential work runs in the browser. No programming or calculus is required.

Quick refresher on measurements
Latency
The time a job takes to finish. A second is 1,000 milliseconds (ms).
Median and p95
Half of outcomes are at or below the median. About 95 in 100 are at or below the 95th percentile (p95).
Cache hit rate
The share of requests served from the cache. A rise from 70% to 80% is 10 percentage points.

Course outline

Follow the units in order if the methods are new. You will build a model, choose how to vary its inputs, diagnose a misleading screen and compare decisions. In the capstone, you will choose assumptions and defend a recommendation using your own runs.
